This table is a summary. [LICENSE](LICENSE) controls and includes attribution/distribution requirements, patent provisions and limitations of liability. The [publisher's explanation](https://polyformproject.org/why-adopt-polyform) confirms that this model reserves commercial licensing while allowing broader noncommercial use. The license is recorded by SPDX as [`PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`](https://spdx.org/licenses/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.html).

The grant covers only repository-owned material and rights the licensor controls. [Third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identify separate dependency licenses and reference-data rights. Required upstream notices and record-level citations must be preserved. This licensing change does not claim to relicense external components or erase independently granted rights.

The earlier initial publication supplied no license grant. This selection supplies the stated noncommercial grant; it does not claim an earlier permissive/open-source license was revoked. Commercial rights are not automatically granted now or retroactively.
